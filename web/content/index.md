Life is short. My philosophy is to learn and try new things whenever possible. I've picked up (and abandoned) many hobbies over time, but three passions have always stood: sports, engineering, and financial markets.

I'm fortunate to combine the latter two and call it a job. I'm a production engineer at Bloomberg's Ticker Plant - the core of its pricing infrastructure - where I'm responsible for the system's tick processing latency, production capacity, and for onboarding new exchanges.

This site is an attempt at solidifying a fourth interest, writing. I hope to share what excites me along the way, whether that'd be personal discoveries, project showcases, or strange little experiments.
